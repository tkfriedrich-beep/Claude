# Skill: Deep Research

Structured methodology for conducting thorough research tasks.

## Process

### 1. Scope Definition
- Clarify the core question
- Define boundaries (time period, geography, industry)
- Identify what "done" looks like
- Set a time/effort budget

### 2. Source Gathering
- **Web search**: Use WebSearch for current information
- **Web fetch**: Use WebFetch to read specific pages in detail
- **Code search**: Use `mcp__github__search_code` for technical research
- **Documents**: Check local files and state/MEMORY.md for prior work

### 3. Multi-Source Triangulation
- Gather at least 3 independent sources per key claim
- Note conflicts between sources
- Assign confidence levels: high (3+ concordant sources), medium (2),
  low (1 or conflicting)

### 4. Synthesis
- Organize findings by theme, not by source
- Highlight surprising or counterintuitive findings
- Call out gaps explicitly

### 5. Output
- Executive summary (3-5 bullet points)
- Detailed findings with confidence levels
- Source list with brief descriptions
- Recommendations with rationale
- Known gaps and suggested follow-up research
