# Ops Audit Template Pack

The problem: solo consultants repeat the same discovery work every engagement and struggle to
look systematic in week one. The pain is real — I feel it myself every audit.

Idea: package my ops-automation audit as a template pack (Notion + spreadsheets + interview
scripts). Customers: independent operations consultants and fractional COOs — a small but
reachable niche via the communities I'm already in.

Monetization: one-time price €149, later a €29/month template subscription with updates.
Validation so far: two consultants asked to buy my checklist after seeing it in a call.
Next: landing page with a waitlist before building anything.

Moat is weak (templates get copied) — the durable advantage would be the update stream and
worked examples from real engagements.
