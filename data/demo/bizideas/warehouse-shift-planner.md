# Warehouse Shift Planner for SME Logistics

Problem: mid-size logistics companies (like Nordwind) still plan warehouse shifts in Excel;
every sick call means 30 minutes of phone tag. Saw the pain first-hand in the discovery call —
the ops lead called Monday planning "the worst 2 hours of the week".

Market: thousands of 50–500 employee logistics/3PL companies in DACH alone; they already pay
for tools (ERP add-ons) and a shift-planning module is a known budget line.

Monetization: per-seat SaaS, ~€4/employee/month; competitors price higher and target
enterprise. Moat: none at the start — wedge is the audit-channel relationship and a
logistics-specific rule library competitors treat as configuration homework.

Validation: none yet beyond one anecdote. Next step: ask three warehouse leads whether they'd
pilot a read-only planner synced from their Excel.
